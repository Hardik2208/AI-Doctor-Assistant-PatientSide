## 🔧 **Technical Requirements**

### **Hardware Requirements per Clinic**

#### **Minimum Setup:**
- **1 Desktop/Laptop** - Core system (Windows/Linux)
- **1 Webcam** - HD quality for video consultations
- **1 Microphone** - External USB mic for clear audio
- **1 Printer** - For prescriptions and reports
- **Internet Connection** - Minimum 2 Mbps (4G/Broadband)

#### **Recommended Setup:**
- **2 Tablets** - Patient-facing interface
- **1 All-in-One PC** - Staff workstation
- **Backup Internet** - Multiple connectivity options
- **UPS System** - Power backup
- **Document Scanner** - For medical records

### **Software Requirements**
- **Operating System**: Windows 10/11, Ubuntu 20+, or Android 8+
- **Browser**: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Edge 90+
- **Storage**: Minimum 8GB free space
- **RAM**: Minimum 4GB (8GB recommended)
- **Network**: Supports offline-first operations

## 🔒 **Risk Management & Mitigation**

### **Technical Risks**

#### **High Risk**
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| **Internet Connectivity Issues** | High | Offline-first design, 4G backup, satellite internet option |
| **Hardware Failures** | Medium | Backup devices, local repair network, replacement protocol |
| **Data Loss** | High | Automated backups, cloud sync, local redundancy |

#### **Medium Risk**
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| **Staff Resistance to Technology** | Medium | Comprehensive training, change management, incentives |
| **Power Outages** | Medium | UPS systems, solar backup, generator protocols |
| **Security Breaches** | High | Encryption, access controls, regular security audits |

#### **Low Risk**
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| **Software Bugs** | Low | Thorough testing, rapid patch deployment, rollback capability |
| **Integration Issues** | Low | Extensive integration testing, phased rollout |
| **User Interface Problems** | Low | User testing, feedback integration, iterative improvement |

### **Operational Risks**

#### **Change Management**
- **Staff Training**: Comprehensive multi-phase training program
- **Process Change**: Gradual transition with parallel systems initially
- **Technology Adoption**: Peer champions and success sharing

#### **Quality Assurance**
- **Data Accuracy**: Validation rules, double-entry for critical data
- **Service Quality**: Regular monitoring, patient feedback, staff assessment
- **Compliance**: Government health regulations, privacy laws, audit trails
